The table export does not infer p-values from rounded paper numbers.
It reads/recomputes the complete exact statistical families from the released
block observations, and records sources in each paper_aggregate directory.
Source reports and the full exact review tables remain alongside the compact
paper exports. LaTeX requires booktabs; complete review tables use longtable.

Figure 5 is exported as paper/figure_05.pdf and .png, with aggregated voxel CSV
and source/summary JSON. Its script is replication/plot_maps.py.
The PDF's Table 1 and Figures 1–4 are conceptual examples, diagrams or simulator
screenshots and are not regenerated by offline numerical evaluation.
Additional learning-curve figures are supplementary rather than PDF Figures 3/4.
